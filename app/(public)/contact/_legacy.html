05 · What happens after you write

Four steps. No surprises.

Whether you fill in the form, email an inbox directly, or apply for early access, the post-message process is the same. We will tell you where you are in the sequence each time we respond.

Step 01
Confirmation, immediately
An automated confirmation email is sent within seconds of submission, with a reference number and a copy of what you sent.
Step 02
Triage & routing, same day
A real person reads it, classifies it, and routes it to the right specialist. We will reach out for clarification only if the routing is ambiguous.
Step 03
Specialist response
A named team member responds with substance — a proposed scope, a meeting offer, a documentation link, an answer. Per the SLA for that topic.
Step 04
Discovery call, if relevant
For sales and service enquiries we propose a 30-minute discovery call to confirm fit and timing — no obligation, no pricing pressure.
06 · Frequently asked

Questions about talking to us.

Answers to the most common “before I send the email” questions. If yours isn't here, send the email anyway.

Will I be put on a marketing nurture sequence after enquiring?
No. Your enquiry is handled by the specialist for that topic, and the conversation runs as long as it needs to. We do not run automated drip campaigns. If you separately tick the newsletter checkbox, you will receive the weekly Insights newsletter and nothing else — you can unsubscribe with one click. Consent for marketing is recorded and POPIA Section 11 compliant.
Can we sign an NDA before discussing the details?
Yes. We have a standard mutual NDA we can send for signature within an hour. We are also happy to sign your standard NDA — legal will mark up unusual departures. Most enterprise procurement processes need an NDA in place before the discovery call, and we treat that as standard practice rather than a hurdle.
Are you B-BBEE certified and CIPC registered?
Yes — SonOfGraig Digital Solutions (Pty) Ltd is CIPC registered and B-BBEE certified. The B-BBEE spend certificate is issued per invoice. The PAIA Manual is published. The Information Officer is registered with the Information Regulator. All commercial documentation is available to your procurement team for supplier on-boarding via trust@sonofgraig.com.
Where is the contact form data stored?
In AWS af-south-1 (Cape Town) — the same region as the rest of our production infrastructure. Form submissions are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), retained for 24 months for follow-up purposes, and deleted on request. Submissions are not shared with third parties; sub-processors that touch this data (Resend for transactional email, Cloudflare for edge protection) are listed in the public sub-processor register on our security page.
Do you take meetings on Teams, Zoom, or Google Meet?
All three. We default to Google Meet because it has the lowest setup cost for the prospect, but we accept any meeting platform your security team approves. Most enterprise security teams in South Africa default to Teams; we are comfortable with that. We will not record meetings without explicit consent. In-person meetings in Pretoria, Johannesburg, and Cape Town are available with one week's notice.
What happens if my enquiry is urgent and outside business hours?
Security disclosures are reachable 24/7 at security@sonofgraig.com — on-call rotation, 1-hour response. Active Enterprise-tier customers reach 24/7 dedicated support via the channel agreed in the SLA — usually a private Slack Connect or a phone bridge. For non-urgent matters outside business hours, expect a response on the next business day in Pretoria.
We are a media outlet — how do we get in touch?
Press, analyst, and broadcast enquiries go to press@sonofgraig.com. Please include your outlet, your deadline, the angle, and whether you are looking for a written quote, an interview, or background. We try to respond within one business day, with deadline-sensitive requests prioritised. Tumiso Graig is the founder spokesperson and is available for interviews on POPIA, enterprise AI in Africa, and the SonOfGraig product strategy.
We're considering acquiring an AI vendor — can we talk?
Conversations of this nature go directly to the founder under NDA. Use legal@sonofgraig.com with the subject line "Strategic conversation" and a brief description. We will respond within one business day to set up an introductory call.
Do you accept bug bounty submissions?
Yes, through coordinated disclosure rather than a public bounty programme at this stage. Send the report to security@sonofgraig.com with reproducible steps and impact analysis. Researchers acting in good faith are credited in our public security advisories where appropriate, and we do not pursue legal action against good-faith research that complies with our responsible disclosure policy. We will publish a formal bug bounty programme once the platform is generally available.
