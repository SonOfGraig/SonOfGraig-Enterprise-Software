Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything your procurement team, CISO, and CFO will ask. Answered honestly.

Why is pricing in ZAR and not USD?

SonOfGraig's cost structure — infrastructure, salaries, operations — is denominated in ZAR. Pricing in USD would create FX risk that we would have to hedge, and that cost would ultimately be passed on to you. By pricing in ZAR, we eliminate that risk entirely. Your software budget does not move when the rand moves. This is a principle, not just a feature.

The products are not GA yet — why commit now?

RAG Studio ships Q4 2026. Agent Builder enters beta Q1 2027. You are not committing to a finished product — you are committing to a founding relationship. Founding customers get 70% off their first year (R5,999/month instead of R19,999/month for Growth), direct access to the founding team, and your real-world requirements shape the product roadmap. The risk is time, not money. If the product does not meet the agreed specifications, founding customers exit without penalty.

Is our data ever sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google servers?

LLM inference (generating answers from retrieved documents) uses Anthropic Claude and optionally Google Gemini. However, all content is passed through our PII scrubber middleware before it leaves your organisational boundary within the platform. What is sent to the LLM API is redacted content, not raw personal information. Your documents are never stored by Anthropic or Google — the API receives only the inference request and returns a response. Both Anthropic and Google have enterprise data processing agreements that prohibit training on your data. Full details are in our Data Processing Agreement.

How does the B-BBEE certification work for procurement?

SonOfGraig is B-BBEE certified. Every invoice includes a B-BBEE spend certificate that your procurement team can use toward your enterprise development and supplier development scorecard targets. As a black-owned, black-managed enterprise, your spend with SonOfGraig qualifies for enhanced recognition under the B-BBEE Codes of Good Practice. Contact us for a copy of our current B-BBEE certificate for your supplier database.

What is the contract term and cancellation policy?

Monthly subscriptions are month-to-month with 30 days written notice for cancellation. Annual subscriptions are billed upfront with a 20% discount and are non-refundable except in cases of material product failure. Enterprise contracts are negotiated individually and typically include 60 or 90 day notice periods. On cancellation, your data is exported to you in a standard format and then securely deleted within 30 days per our data retention policy.

Can we get a free trial before committing?

We do not offer a self-serve free trial. Instead, we offer a scoped proof of concept engagement through our services pricing — this gives you a production-ready implementation with your actual data and requirements, rather than a sandboxed demo that does not reflect your real environment. This is more valuable than a free trial for enterprise buyers and ensures the first thing you see is the product working with your data, not generic sample data. Contact us to discuss a scoped PoC.

Does the platform support Microsoft Entra / Azure AD SSO?

Yes. Growth and Enterprise tiers include SSO via SAML 2.0. We support Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure Active Directory), Okta, Google Workspace, and any SAML 2.0 compliant identity provider. OIDC support is on the roadmap for Q1 2027. Starter tier does not include SSO — users authenticate directly via email/password with MFA.
